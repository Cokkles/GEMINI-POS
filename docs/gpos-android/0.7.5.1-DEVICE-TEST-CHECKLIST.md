# AEGIS Android 0.7.5.1 — Device Test Checklist

Use the verified `AEGIS-Android-0.7.5.1-nutrition-reliability.apk` over the existing installation.
Do not uninstall first; the checkpoint certificate is intentionally stable.

## Install and session

- [ ] Android accepts the APK as an update to `com.cokkles.gpos`.
- [ ] Existing theme, local notes, and authenticated account continuity remain intact.
- [ ] Opening the app shows cached content immediately while protected reads refresh quietly.
- [ ] A queued capture resumes after a successful foreground Google renewal.

## Nutrition capture

- [ ] Submit one simple item such as `banana` and receive a confirmed result.
- [ ] Submit `Oikos Pro vanilla protein drink, Nature's Bakery whole wheat fig bar` and confirm both foods are logged separately.
- [ ] Submit `Black and Bleu burger from Williams Gourmet Kitchen` and confirm a sourced or component-estimated result is returned rather than a zero-macro fallback.
- [ ] Confirm the result shows calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, saturated fat, fiber, sugar, sodium, and cholesterol when available.
- [ ] Confirm estimated restaurant food reports confidence, lookup depth, assumptions, and conservative adjustment.
- [ ] Confirm no side, drink, or extra sauce is added unless stated.

## Queue and deduplication

- [ ] Immediately after capture, continue navigating and submitting other notes without waiting.
- [ ] Watch the receipt change from Queued to Submitting to Confirmed.
- [ ] During a transient Gemini capacity failure, confirm retries occur at approximately 60 and 120 seconds.
- [ ] After three failed attempts, confirm one failure notification appears and manual retry becomes available.
- [ ] Replaying the same Capture ID does not append a duplicate nutrition row.
- [ ] Close and reopen AEGIS while a capture is queued; confirm it remains present and later resumes.

## Spreadsheet and cross-client regression

- [ ] Existing nutrition columns A:J retain their names, order, and values.
- [ ] Expanded nutrition/provenance values appear only in additive columns K:X.
- [ ] The older PWA can still log `/calories` successfully.
- [ ] Windows can still read nutrition and HORIZON without schema errors.
- [ ] Today’s KINETIC calorie total includes the confirmed rows exactly once.

## Report back

Record the exact food text, final receipt state, displayed message, attempt count, approximate duration,
whether the sheet row appeared, and whether the row was duplicated. A screenshot of Alerts & Receipts
plus the corresponding sheet row is sufficient for failure diagnosis.
