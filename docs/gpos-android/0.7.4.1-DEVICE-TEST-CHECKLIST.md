# AEGIS Android 0.7.4.1 Device Test Checklist

1. Install 0.7.4.1 over 0.7.4 without uninstalling.
2. Confirm Google connection and existing protected local data remain available.
3. Submit one Meal / Calories item and confirm a non-zero macro row appears in the canonical nutrition ledger.
4. Submit two or more comma-separated foods and confirm each food receives a parsed nutrition row.
5. Submit foods on separate lines and confirm they remain parseable without bullet characters being inserted.
6. Confirm the Android receipt becomes Confirmed only after the backend returns a completed nutrition result.
7. If Gemini fails, confirm Android reports an unconfirmed meal and does not silently label the zero-macro fallback successful.
8. Do not retry an ambiguous failed meal until the canonical ledger has been checked for a partial or fallback write.
9. Smoke-test Note, Journal, and Receipt capture for regressions.
10. Confirm the installed app reports version 0.7.4.1-dev (version code 17).
