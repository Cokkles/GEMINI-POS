# AEGIS Android 0.7.4 Device Test Checklist

1. Install over the accepted 0.7.3 build without uninstalling; confirm Notes, theme, Task preferences, and pending receipts remain present.
2. Cold-open AEGIS with a previously connected account. Cached Home content should appear without a blocking Google sign-in screen.
3. Confirm the header transitions from local/offline continuity to Connected without navigating away from the screen you opened.
4. Close and reopen several times during a valid session. Google account selection should not repeatedly appear.
5. Leave AEGIS unused long enough for the prior ID token to expire, then reopen. Confirm Credential Manager renews the authorized account automatically when possible.
6. If Google requires interaction, confirm AEGIS retains cached private data and presents Reconnect instead of treating the account as explicitly signed out.
7. While offline, create/edit a Task, complete another Task, and promote or dismiss a Follow-up. Confirm each remains locally reflected and queued.
8. Reconnect after token expiry, open AEGIS, and confirm the queued actions resume after authorization without being entered again.
9. Queue a Gemini-dependent Capture, allow authorization to lapse before retry, then reopen AEGIS. Confirm the Capture remains queued and resumes rather than being marked failed solely for token expiry.
10. Check that at most one `AEGIS needs Google authorization` notification is present and that opening it returns to Settings & Diagnostics.
11. After successful renewal, confirm the authorization notification is removed and Alerts/Receipts show the eventual operation outcomes.
12. Revoke AEGIS/Google account authorization externally, reopen, and confirm an explicit reconnect action is required while cached data remains protected.
13. Explicitly Sign out. Confirm protected local data and queued work are cleared and the app does not silently sign back in.
14. Sign in again and smoke-test Home, Tasks, Notes/Capture, Calendar, News, Follow-ups, Alerts, and theme persistence.
