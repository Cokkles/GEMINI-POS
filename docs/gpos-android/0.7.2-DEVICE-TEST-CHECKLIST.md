# AEGIS Android 0.7.2 Device Test Checklist

## Upgrade and navigation

- Install over 0.7.1 without uninstalling; confirm sign-in continuity and Running Notes text remain present.
- Confirm the bottom destination says Notes and opens Running Notes first with one-time capture below.
- Open an older Running Notes shortcut/deep link and confirm it reaches the combined Notes page.
- Confirm Ask AEGIS is absent from More while all other daily destinations remain reachable.

## Notes and capture

- Edit Running Notes, leave the screen, return, and restart the app; confirm autosaved text remains.
- Sync a Running Notes section and confirm a new section starts only after backend confirmation.
- Submit one-time Note, Journal, single-item Meal, comma-separated Meal, and Receipt captures.
- Confirm the confirmations link opens Alerts & Receipts and history starts collapsed.

## Home customization

- Expand Home task-list settings, hide Grocery, and confirm Grocery tasks disappear while other lists stay separately labeled.
- Restart the app and confirm the visibility choice persists locally; re-enable the list.
- Expand Follow-ups, Promote one to Tasks, and confirm canonical Tasks refresh.
- Dismiss another follow-up and confirm it disappears and does not return after refresh.
- Confirm the HORIZON strip and quote are compact and open/advance correctly.

## Tasks and News

- Add and edit a task; tap Choose due day and select a date from the calendar dialog.
- Remove a due day and confirm the saved task refreshes without a due value.
- In News Headliners settings, move a section up/down; leave and return and confirm the order persists.
- Toggle Deals eligibility and confirm the Headliners result changes without removing Deals from its full section.

## Regression

- Verify Calendar Home shortcut, all bottom tabs, Google reconnect return-to-screen behavior, collapsed histories, themes, and notification deep links.
- Confirm explicit Sign out clears identity-bound protected local data; reconnect errors do not.
