# AEGIS Android 0.7.4.2 — Capture Transport Reliability

## Outcome

Meal, note, journal, and receipt captures are accepted into the encrypted local ledger immediately and dispatched by WorkManager. Apps Script/Gemini capture calls may wait up to 185 seconds without holding the Android capture UI open.

## Diagnostic contract

- `CONFIRMED`: the backend returned a recognized success response.
- `CAPTURE_UNCONFIRMED_RESULT`: the backend returned a fallback such as `Macros pending`; Android does not claim success.
- `CAPTURE_TIMEOUT`: no response arrived within 185 seconds. Write state is unknown.
- `CAPTURE_NETWORK_FAILURE`: the connection ended without confirmation. Write state is unknown.
- Backend error codes are preserved on the local receipt when supplied.

The diagnostics are visible under More → Alerts & Receipts → Recent confirmations.

## Duplicate-safety boundary

Android never automatically retries an ambiguous timeout or network interruption because Apps Script 2.8.0 does not advertise durable `submission_id` deduplication. Automatic 60/120-second retries remain limited to Gemini capacity failures carrying `AEGIS_CAPTURE_RELIABILITY_V1`, `retryable=true`, and `write_state=NOT_STARTED`.

## Shared backend and delivery

- Apps Script baseline: 2.8.0; unchanged by this Android patch.
- Package: `com.cokkles.gpos`
- Version: `0.7.4.2-dev` (`versionCode 18`)
- Every verified Android test APK is copied to the established Google Drive `GPOS-Android` folder after its package, signer, size, and SHA-256 are recorded.
