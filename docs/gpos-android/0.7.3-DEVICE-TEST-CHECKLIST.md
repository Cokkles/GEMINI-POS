# AEGIS Android 0.7.3 Device Test Checklist

1. Install over 0.7.2 and confirm saved Notes, task visibility preferences, and the connected account remain present.
2. Create and edit a Task. The dialog should close immediately and the task should show the local change with `sync pending` feedback.
3. Make another edit within two minutes. Confirm the app remains responsive and automatic synchronization waits for the latest quiet period.
4. Complete, delete, and restore Tasks; confirm no full-screen loading state blocks navigation.
5. Promote two follow-ups back-to-back. Each should leave the active list immediately and accept the second action without waiting.
6. Dismiss another follow-up and confirm it cannot be acted on twice.
7. Wait at least two minutes with connectivity. Refresh and confirm the changes appear in Google Tasks and AEGIS follow-ups.
8. Use Sync now and confirm pending operations flush immediately.
9. Stage a change offline, close AEGIS, restore connectivity, and confirm background synchronization eventually completes.
10. Check Alerts & Receipts for pending/confirmed status. Any failed ambiguous write must show as failed rather than being duplicated.
11. Explicitly sign out and confirm protected queued work is cleared with the account-bound local workspace.
12. Open Appearance, select several new dark palettes and Flexoki Light, and confirm cards, text, controls, dialogs, and status colors remain readable.
13. Close and reopen AEGIS and confirm the selected theme persists.
