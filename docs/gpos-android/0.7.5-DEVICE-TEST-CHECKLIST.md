# AEGIS Android 0.7.5 Device Test Checklist

1. Install 0.7.5 over 0.7.4.2 without uninstalling. Confirm `0.7.5-dev` and version code 19.
2. Open AEGIS while the saved Google token is still valid. Confirm cached Home content appears immediately and no Google account dialog opens.
3. Close and reopen AEGIS a second time. Confirm the header reaches Connected without a full-screen wait and automatic restoration does not leave the top loading bar running across screens.
4. Use the manual refresh button. Confirm its progress indication appears, completes, and does not remain stuck after switching screens.
5. Create or edit a task, then leave the app. Reopen after two or more minutes and confirm the local change remains visible and reaches Google Tasks when connectivity is available.
6. Promote or dismiss several Follow Ups back-to-back. Confirm each item changes locally without allowing duplicate taps and the queued operations finish.
7. Submit a multi-item Calories capture such as `protein shake, banana`. Confirm the receipt reaches Confirmed and nutrition appears after refresh; this preserves the working 0.7.4.2 transport fix.
8. With notification permission enabled, create a timed Calendar event roughly 65 minutes ahead and refresh Calendar. Close AEGIS and confirm the 60-minute and 15-minute reminders arrive. Android battery optimization can delay WorkManager delivery.
9. Create another timed event, refresh Calendar, then edit or delete it and refresh again. Confirm the old scheduled reminder does not appear.
10. Let Google authorization expire or reproduce Reconnect Required. Confirm cached private data and pending local mutations remain intact, an authentication-attention notification can reopen AEGIS, and successful renewal resumes queued work.
11. Explicitly sign out. Confirm private cached content disappears, pending protected ledgers are cleared according to the existing sign-out rule, and Calendar reminders no longer fire.
12. Regression-check Home, Calendar, Tasks, Notes/Capture, News, Nutrition, Alerts, Follow Ups, Finances, and System navigation.
