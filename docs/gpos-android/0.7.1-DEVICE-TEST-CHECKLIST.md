# AEGIS Android 0.7.1 Device Test Checklist

## Upgrade and session continuity

- Install over 0.7.0 and confirm existing protected Running Notes remain available.
- Allow a session to expire or simulate an authorization failure; confirm the header says Reconnect and cached private screens remain present.
- Tap Reconnect from the original screen, complete Google confirmation, and confirm navigation remains on that screen.
- Explicitly sign out; confirm protected local notes, queued mutations, and capture history are removed.

## Capture reliability

- Submit `eggs, toast, coffee` as Meal / Calories and confirm all three items are represented.
- Submit a single meal and confirm its wording is preserved.
- Confirm an ordinary Note containing commas is not split.
- When Apps Script reports high volume without the typed reliability contract, confirm Android does not retry automatically.
- After a future certified backend response, confirm retries occur near 60 and 120 seconds, stop after attempt three, and produce only a final failure notification.

## Daily UI

- Expand and recollapse Completed Tasks, Nutrition history, Meal history, Running Notes recovery history, and Recent confirmations.
- Confirm these historical sections start collapsed after opening their screens.
- Confirm Running Notes offers Checkpoint and Copy but no Share button.
