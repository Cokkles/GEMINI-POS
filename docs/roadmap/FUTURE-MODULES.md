# Future Modules

## SENTINEL-ATLAS

Status: `PLANNED`

Purpose: investment, portfolio, asset, market, and wealth-growth intelligence within the SENTINEL financial family.

Planned capabilities include portfolio state, allocation, performance, contribution tracking, market/security research, opportunity screening, investment theses, risk/concentration analysis, scenario modeling, and long-term wealth planning.

Boundary: SENTINEL-ATLAS consumes approved SENTINEL-FIN financial state rather than independently determining household financial truth or investable cash.

## COMPASS

Status: `PLANNED`

Purpose: career strategy, opportunity tracking, mentorship, skill/evidence tracking, accomplishments, growth planning, and career decision support.

NotebookLM career-planning material may later become reference/source material, but existence of reference material must not silently create canonical career state.

## GPOS command/control plane

Status: `PLANNED`

A canonical command registry should eventually define command identity, display name, domain owner, intent, aliases, arguments, read/write scope, confirmation requirements, input/output contracts, and supported surfaces.

Supported future surfaces may include AEGIS, chat, Tasker, widgets, voice, and automated test clients.
