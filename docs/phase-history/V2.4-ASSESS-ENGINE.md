## TypedClaim boundary

Every substantive analytical statement is structurally represented as one of:

- `FACT`
- `SELF_REPORT`
- `PATTERN`
- `HYPOTHESIS`
- `RECOMMENDATION`

Cross-domain coincidence cannot be promoted to causal fact without evidence. Open tasks cannot automatically become executive-dysfunction claims; project activity cannot automatically become hyperfocus; historical Scratchpad content cannot become active state.

## Five sections

1. Somatic / Cognitive Pulse
2. Work Pattern / Hyperfocus Analysis
3. Career / Professional Steering
4. Tactile / Leisure / Family Alignment
5. Immediate Moves

Immediate Moves requires exactly three recommendation claims and has zero task side effects.

## Boundary behavior

- no persistent COMPASS state;
- no production task/goal/journal/ledger mutation;
- renderer formats claims but may not invent new analysis.
